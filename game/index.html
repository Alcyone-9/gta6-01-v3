How to play

Follow the gold beacon and minimap blip to each job giver, press F, read the dialogue, then chase the marker. Stealing parked cars and crashing raises your wanted stars: cruisers ram, officers chase on foot, roadblocks and spike strips wait at four stars, and a searchlight helicopter joins at five. Slow down near police and the BUSTED meter fills. Golden cassettes glow at corners all over the city; each pays cash, all ten pay a collector bonus. The day turns to night and back roughly every four minutes: lamps, windows and headlights light the streets after dark.
