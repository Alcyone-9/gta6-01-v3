An original open-world crime game that runs entirely from static files in a desktop browser. No assets are loaded over the network; the city, the music and every sound are generated by the code in this repository.

## Story

Vera Solano is the fastest courier on the Coral City docks, and everybody knows it. When Marina Vale's cafe cooler dies on the hottest morning of the season, Vera takes the run — and one job becomes ten. A stranded ferry mechanic, three scattered cargo crates, a vaccine cooler racing the clock, a retired racer's sunset circuit, a sculpture that cannot take a scratch, a singer's stolen royalties with the police already rolling, a tow-yard rescue, a night-market gear hunt, and finally the whole tide-festival fund in a single case, with every crew in the city watching. Finish the chain and Coral City is hers; get caught with the BUSTED meter full, or flatline, and the fines come straight out of her pocket.

## The city

Coral City is a 5x5 block grid of pastel towers with lit windows, neon signs and varied roofs, wrapped by a beach and open water to the east. Palms, benches, hydrants, billboards, market stalls, harbor cranes, lifeguard towers, a central fountain and moored boats fill the streets. Named districts — Harbor Quarter, Marina Row, Downtown, Gallery Mile, Coral General and more — are announced as you cross them. A full day-night cycle turns roughly every four minutes: the sun and moon cross the sky, fog and sky colour shift through golden hour, and at night the street lamps, building windows and car headlights take over.

## Features

- On-foot third-person play and arcade driving with gas, brake, steering, handbrake drift, collisions and damage smoke.
- Five civilian vehicle types — sedan, sport coupe, SUV, pickup and van — plus a police cruiser and a tow truck, each with its own top speed, acceleration, handling and health.
- Traffic AI that holds lanes, slows for intersections, brakes for obstacles and honks; parked cars all over the city that Vera can simply take.
- Twenty-six pedestrians strolling the sidewalks who step aside from fast cars and flee from danger.
- A wanted system from 0 to 5 stars: heat and decay, pursuing cruisers, foot officers who chase Vera on foot or when her car dies, roadblocks with spike strips at four stars, a searchlight helicopter at five, and a BUSTED meter that fills while police are close and Vera is slow — arrest means respawning at CCPD HQ with a fine, and zero health means Coral General with a hospital bill.
- A ten-job story campaign with named original characters and dialogue boxes: timed delivery, pickup, crate collection, a checkpoint race, a no-damage delivery, an escape-the-police delivery, tow duty, a gear hunt and a multi-stage finale.
- Ten hidden golden cassettes that glow and spin across the city, each paying cash, with a collector bonus for the full set and hints in the code for the stubborn.
- A full HUD: cash, Vera's health, car health, wanted stars, clock, district and compass readout, objective banner, dialogue UI, toasts, a BUSTED meter, damage vignette, speedometer and a live canvas minimap with roads, water, traffic, police, helicopter, cassette and job-marker blips.
- Procedural WebAudio throughout: a looping bassline, arpeggio, kick, snare and hats groove, an engine note tied to speed, distance-based sirens, horns, doors, crashes, pickups, cash, checkpoint pings, footsteps, splashes and success and failure stingers. No audio files.
- Speed feel: the camera field of view widens as the car accelerates and eases behind the car while driving.
- Start screen with story and controls, pause and help overlays, a story-complete screen, and a localStorage save game with Continue and autosave after every job.

## Controls

- W A S D or arrows: move on foot / drive
- Shift: run (drains stamina) — Mouse: orbit the camera
- Space: handbrake while driving
- E: enter or leave a car, advance dialogue
- F: talk to a job giver / start a job
- H: horn — M: toggle music — P or Esc: pause

## Running locally

Serve the game folder with any static file server, for example:

    node serve.ts

then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Screenshot poses used for the entry images live at ?shot=cover, ?shot=drive, ?shot=chase and ?shot=mission.

## Handing in

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

copies game/ and screenshots/ to the output directory and writes dossier.json with the bundled make.fun library.

## Structure

game/src is split one job per file: config (tuning), random (seeded RNG), input, audio (procedural sound), world (city and light rig), vehicles (factory, physics, traffic), pedestrians, player, police (wanted system), missions (the ten-job chain), collectibles (golden cassettes), hud (DOM HUD and minimap), save (localStorage), shots (screenshot poses) and main (boot, loop, state machine). three.js is vendored under game/vendor/three/ as its minified build.
